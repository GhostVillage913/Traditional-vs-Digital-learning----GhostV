CAT Research Project

Digital vs Traditional Learning

I explored how digital learning and traditional classroom learning each affect access, motivation, understanding, and fairness for learners in South Africa.

View Full Research

Research task steps

How I completed the research tasks

The report compares traditional and digital learning using survey results, research sources, and five outcome measures. The document does not specify the survey sample size or score scale, so results below are shown exactly as recorded.

01

Define the task

I focused the project on how learning methods affect access, engagement, motivation, retention, and assessment for South African learners.

02

Research both approaches

I examined the strengths and limits of classroom teaching and digital tools, including access, affordability, engagement, retention, and cyber safety.

03

Organize survey results

I arranged the reported learner results into five profiles: Traditional, Slightly less traditional, Average, Slightly more digital, and Digital.

04

Compare and recommend

I compared each group on five outcome measures, identified the strongest recorded results, and used them to form a practical recommendation.

Project data

What the comparison showed

The highest recorded score for each measure came from different learner groups. That pattern supports a balanced approach rather than assuming one method works best for everyone.

Engagement 5

Highest for the slightly more digital-based group.

Motivation 5

Highest for the slightly less traditional-based group.

Technical challenges 5

Most noticeable in the average group.

Memory retention 7

Highest for the average group.

Assessment quality 4

Highest in both traditional-based and digital-based groups.

Category analysis

Research areas explored

Access & Infrastructure

Not every learner has a stable internet connection, data plan, or compatible device. This creates a digital divide that affects fairness in education.

Traditional Learning

Classroom learning encourages direct communication, structured routines, teacher supervision, and clear face-to-face support.

Digital Learning

Online platforms promote flexibility, self-paced study, video-based teaching, and instant access to educational resources.

Safety & Ethics

Digital learning must include online safety, privacy awareness, digital responsibility, and secure browsing habits.
