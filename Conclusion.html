Final conclusion

Conclusion

A blended learning approach is the most effective and equitable way to combine classroom support with the flexibility of digital learning for South African learners.

Conclusion

This investigation shows that South African education cannot rely exclusively on either traditional or digital learning. Digital learning provides flexibility, independence, and interactive resources, but unequal access to devices, internet connectivity, and affordable data limits who can benefit. Traditional learning offers structure, teacher guidance, immediate feedback, and social interaction, although it may not provide the same flexibility and digital engagement.

The report's survey table records engagement at 5 for the slightly more digital-based group, motivation at 5 for the slightly less traditional-based group, technical challenges at 5 and memory retention at 7 for the average group, and assessment quality at 4 for both the traditional-based and digital-based groups. These are reproduced as reported scores; the document does not specify their scale. The results show different strengths across learner groups rather than one approach leading on every measure.

The report recommends integrating digital tools into traditional classrooms, supported by fair access to data and devices, teacher training, cyber-safety education, and balanced assessment. It concludes that this blended approach is the most effective and equitable solution for South African learners.

Recommendations for implementation

  • Integrate digital tools into traditional classrooms.
  • Invest in affordable data, internet access, and shared devices.
  • Train teachers to use digital resources alongside classroom methods.
  • Teach digital literacy, privacy, and cyber safety.
  • Balance digital practice with fair classroom assessment and teacher feedback.
  • Involve parents and communities in supporting learning.

Combining classroom support with digital flexibility can strengthen engagement and collaboration while preparing learners for responsible technology use. Addressing access and safety is essential for this model to be inclusive and sustainable.
